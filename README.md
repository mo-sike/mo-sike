### 🧪 things i've made

🩹 **[SelfHealing_UI_Automation](https://github.com/mo-sike/SelfHealing_UI_Automation)**
Show it two screenshots, "before" and "after" an app update. It spots what moved, vanished or changed colour, then tries to patch your broken tests on its own. Under the hood there's a lot going on (YOLOv8, CLIP, graph matching, a bit of entropy), but the idea is simple: **tests that heal themselves**. When it isn't sure, it asks a human instead of quietly guessing. Very polite.

🖱️ **[mouseketeer_bot](https://github.com/mo-sike/mouseketeer_bot)**
My laptop kept falling asleep while my AI agents were mid-task. So I built a tiny mouse that wiggles and clicks to keep it awake. Problem solved, mouse employed. 🐭
